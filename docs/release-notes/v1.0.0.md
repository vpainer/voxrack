**VoxRack turns your Windows microphone into a studio voice chain.** EQ, multiband compression, compressor, gate,
de-esser, autotune, delay, reverb and a limiter run inside Windows itself — so OBS, Discord, Zoom, Telegram and
every other app get the processed sound from your usual microphone. No virtual cables, no extra devices.

## ⬇️ Download

**[VoxRack-1.0.0-win-x64.zip](https://github.com/vpainer/voxrack/releases/download/v1.0.0/VoxRack-1.0.0-win-x64.zip)** — Windows 10 (2004+) / 11, x64. Nothing else to install.

1. Unzip anywhere and run `VoxRack.exe`.
2. Pick your microphone and press **Connect** (Windows asks for admin rights once; sound drops for ~2 s).
3. Choose a preset — *Podcast*, *Radio*, *Stream* or *Calls* — and tune it by ear.

> The app isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may show *Windows protected your PC* → **More info → Run anyway**.

## ✨ What's inside

* **A chain you can rearrange** — drag modules into any order; input/output first, limiter last.
* **Multiband like the pros** — bands right on the live spectrum, a floating panel with the band's knobs.
* **See every move** — live spectrum before/after, gain-reduction history, compressor transfer curve, pitch track.
* **Autotune** that keeps formants — key, scale, speed (0 = robot), amount, transpose.
* **MATCH** — make one microphone sound like another from a 15-second recording.
* **TAKE** — loop a recorded phrase through the chain while you tweak; A/B with the dry take.
* **MON**, presets, per-microphone settings, Shure MV7 LED modes and touch lock.
* A native **Windows 11** app: Mica, light/dark theme and accent from Windows, English and Russian.

## 🇷🇺 Коротко по-русски

VoxRack встраивает студийную обработку голоса прямо в микрофон Windows: эквалайзер, многополосный компрессор,
гейт, де-эссер, автотюн, делей, реверб, лимитер. OBS, Discord, Zoom и Telegram получают уже обработанный звук —
без виртуальных кабелей. Скачай архив выше, распакуй, запусти `VoxRack.exe`, выбери микрофон и нажми
**Подключить**. Интерфейс на русском включится сам, если Windows на русском (или в меню `⋯ → Язык`).
